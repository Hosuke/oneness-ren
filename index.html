    仁 · 二人

    Two walk together.

    一

    从人从二Person and two

    「仁，親也。从人从二。」

    — 許慎《說文解字》

    “Rén: closeness. From ‘person’ and ‘two’.”
    — Shuowen Jiezi

    「樊遲問仁。子曰：愛人。」

    — 《論語·顏淵》

    “Fan Chi asked about rén. The Master said: ‘Love others.’”
    — Analects 12.22

    仁不在一個人身上，在兩個人之間。Rén is not in one person; it is between two.

    二

    同行二人Dōgyō ninin

    四國八十八所，一條千餘公里的環行之路。遍路者（お遍路さん）披白衣、戴菅笠。白衣原是死裝束——上路之時，已將此身放下。

    Eighty-eight temples on a circuit of more than a thousand kilometres around Shikoku. Pilgrims wear white and a sedge hat. The white robe was once a burial garment: setting out, one has already let this life go.

    笠上寫著「同行二人」：一人上路，弘法大師空海始終相伴。手中的金剛杖，被視為大師的化身。

    On the hat: dōgyō ninin, “two travelling together.” Walking alone, one walks with Kōbō Daishi Kūkai. The staff in one’s hand is regarded as the Daishi himself.

    一人上路，從來是兩個人。One sets out alone, and is always two.

    參考 Sources · 四國 Shikoku · 平等寺 Byōdōji

    三

    倒轉巴別Babel, reversed

    「那時，天下人的口音、言語都是一樣。」

    — 創世記 11:1，和合本

    “And the whole earth was of one language, and of one speech.”
    — Genesis 11:1, KJV

    「因為耶和華在那裡變亂天下人的言語，使眾人分散在全地上，所以那城名叫巴別。」

    — 創世記 11:9，和合本

    “Therefore is the name of it called Babel; because the Lord did there confound the language of all the earth: and from thence did the Lord scatter them abroad upon the face of all the earth.”
    — Genesis 11:9, KJV

    不是回到同一種語言，
    是每一種語言都指向同一件事。Not a return to one language — every language pointing to the same thing.
