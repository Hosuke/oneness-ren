本字，是「仁」本身。對譯，是確曾用來譯「仁」的詞；近義，是別種文化裡相鄰而不相同的觀念。我們記下它們的距離：尊重差異，而非抹平差異。

The character itself is rén. A translation is a word used to translate rén; a neighbouring idea belongs to another tradition and is not its equivalent. We name the distance between them: respecting differences, rather than erasing them.
